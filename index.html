Eligibility notes come from reading each linked Notice of Race, using the most recent edition posted at the time. Rules can change year to year, so re-check an event's NOR once next year's edition is published.

Insurance: US Sailing membership doesn't include third-party liability cover. Most events here require it, and the BC events set a USD minimum for US boats.

Flying Scot racing in the region is thin. The Flying Scot class lists Pacific District fleets at Columbia Basin Sailing Club (Richland), Lake Washington and Sequim Bay, but the PT Small Boat Regatta is the only annual regatta found with a dedicated Scot start. Sequim Bay YC's one-design regatta for Thistles, Scots, Lightnings and 505s last appears on Regatta Network in 2021.

Not annual, so not listed: the 505 Pacific Coast Championships (Bellingham YC in 2025) and Bellingham's Dale Jepsen One-Design regatta, which hasn't been held since 2021.
